Onboarding verification: 3 days → 15 minutes

Identity, liveness, and watchlist screening used to wait on humans and serial vendor calls. I designed the backend so most users finish KYC/AML in a single session.

The problem

Manual review queues and one-vendor-at-a-time checks meant a user could wait days to fund or transact. That is a product problem and a compliance problem: drop-off on one side, incomplete screening on the other.

The shape of the system

  • Plaid IDV for identity and bank-linked signals.
  • Persona for biometric liveness and document capture.
  • Bond / Atelio for banking/card rails where the product needed them.
  • Oracle OWS + OEDQ later in the stack for watchlist screening, profiling, cleansing, and de-duplication.

The NestJS services did not “call a vendor and hope.” Each vendor was an adapter behind a verification state machine: pending, needs-retry, manual-review, passed, failed. Webhooks and polling both had to land on the same idempotent status row.

What actually made it fast

  • Run independent vendor checks in parallel instead of a chain.
  • Idempotent processors so a duplicate webhook cannot double-create a customer or skip screening.
  • A small manual-review queue only for the residual ~2%, not for the default path.
  • Clear failure reasons back to the client so users retry the right step instead of restarting KYC.

Related money path

The same platform scores cashflow from 3–6 months of banking data (Plaid/Bond) so advance eligibility is minutes, not 48 hours — and posts disbursements/repayments/card auths on a double-entry ledger at 5M+ monthly transactions.
